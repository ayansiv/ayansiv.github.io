Ayan Sivaram

I'm an NYU student studying finance, statistics, and philosophy. I also spent a year in London at UCL.

I'm a fellow in the Supervised Program for Alignment Research and a research assistant at the Forecasting Research Institute, where I work on expert elicitation for AI risk surveys. I'm writing a senior thesis on monetary policy under transformative AI, and a paper on export controls.

This summer I was a trading intern at JP Morgan and attended the Hoover Institution Summer Policy Boot Camp. Before that I was an investment analyst at One Madison Group, a multi-strategy crossover fund.

I volunteer with Asian American Dream and with Sponsors for Educational Opportunity as an interviewer for their SEO Scholars college access program.

  • Organizing. Co-founded the NYU AI Safety Association. Also running The Giving Pitch, a grantmaking competition across universities.
  • Forecasting & prediction markets. AI forecasters and begging people not to use point estimates. Also, how can we get people to hedge their emotions?

  • Economics. What does monetary policy look like if AI breaks Okun's Law? Also, automation makes the natural rate of interest non-stationary, which quietly breaks Taylor rules.
